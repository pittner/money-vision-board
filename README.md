# Money Vision Board 2027

36 money and financial goals in 6 groups (saving, debt, budget and spending, earning more, investing and future, money mindset). Each goal has why it matters and a first step for this week, plus 12 honest money questions and a 45-minute plan.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/money-vision-board/

- Press a goal and it opens in the [free vision board maker](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/free-vision-board-maker/?mode=wallpaper&layout=text&utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=money_2027) with the first step under it; save it as a 1080x1920 lock screen so you see it every time you are about to buy something
- Or type your own goal and first step
- No signup, nothing uploaded, MIT licensed

Rebuild: `python3 build.py` (no dependencies)
